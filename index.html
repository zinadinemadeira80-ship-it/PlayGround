Rugby, arcade style

  • Pass backwards. A forward pass turns the ball over via scrum.
  • Kick with purpose. Tap for a grubber, hold for a punt. Charging while moving slows you down.
  • Win the breakdown. After a tackle, tap the tackle/pass key to win the ruck. Get bodies there first.
  • Time the sidestep. Press L just before contact to slip a tackle — it costs stamina.
  • Chase kicks. The ball carrier changes after a kick; the nearest chaser is yours.
  • Touch = lineout. Knock-on = scrum. Win the timing bar to keep possession.
  • Score tries (5) and conversions (2). Nail the conversion power zone.

Set plays

Open the Playbook → Set Plays tab. Pick a teammate, drag them into position, then click upfield to sketch their run. Assign a first receiver. In a match, hit 1–4 while carrying: your teammates explode onto their lines and the first receiver takes an automatic pass.

Equip up to four plays in the Matchday Keys panel. Plays are saved to your browser automatically.

Squad building

Every player has six attributes and a trait. Spend the point budget across ten players — pace wins the outside, strength and tackling win the breakdown, handling keeps the ball alive.

Your club identity, squad, tactics and set plays persist locally. Reset the squad any time from the Squad Builder.
